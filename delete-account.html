Delete your account

VibeFlick is developed by VibeFlick Gx (H.M Avishka Dilshan), Sri Lanka. You can delete your account and its data from inside the app, or by emailing us if you can’t open the app.

Delete from the app

This is the fastest way. It takes about a minute.

  1. Open VibeFlick and log in If you set a PIN, you will be asked to enter it first.
  2. Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account
  3. Confirm it’s you Enter your password, type DELETE, then confirm on the final screen.

Can’t open the app? Request deletion by email

Use this if you forgot your password, signed up with a social login, or no longer have the app installed.

Send an email to support@vibeflick.com from the email address linked to your account, with this subject line:

Account Deletion Request – VibeFlick

Include your VibeFlick username. We will verify that the account is yours and process your request.

Email a deletion request

What happens next

Deletion is not instant. We keep a short recovery window in case you change your mind.

  • Right away
    Your account is deactivated and scheduled for deletion. Your profile and content stop being visible to other users, and you are signed out.
  • Within 30 days
    Log in again to recover your account and cancel the deletion.
  • After 30 days
    Your account and the data listed below are permanently deleted. This cannot be undone.

What is deleted and what is kept

When the 30 days end, this is what happens to your data.

Permanently deleted

  • Profile information: name, username, email, phone number
  • Photos, videos and voice clips
  • Posts, thoughts and comments
  • Followers and connections
  • Saved items and favorites
  • Messages
  • Device and usage data linked to your account

May be kept for a limited time

  • Limited records needed to prevent fraud, investigate security issues, or meet legal obligations
  • Backup copies, which are overwritten or removed within 90 days of deletion

Content you shared publicly and that other people have already downloaded or reposted outside VibeFlick is not under our control.

Questions?

Write to support@vibeflick.com and we will help. You can also read how we handle your data in our Privacy Policy.
