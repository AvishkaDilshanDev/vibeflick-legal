VibeFlick Community Guidelines

Effective Date: September 2026

Building a safe, respectful, and creative community

🔞

You must be at least 13 years old (or 18, where required by local law) to use VibeFlick.

Welcome to VibeFlick! These Community Guidelines exist to keep our platform safe, respectful, and enjoyable for everyone who shares and discovers videos, photos, voice clips, and thoughts here. By using VibeFlick, you agree to follow these guidelines in addition to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

We want VibeFlick to be a place where creativity thrives without fear of harassment, hate, or harm. Every rule below exists to protect our community — please take a moment to read through them.

1Prohibited Content

The following types of content are strictly prohibited on VibeFlick — whether posted as a video, photo, voice clip, thought, or comment. Violations may result in content removal, account restrictions, or permanent bans, depending on severity.

🚫 Nudity & Explicit Content

No sexually explicit content, pornography, or non-consensual intimate imagery. This includes nudity involving minors in any form, which will be immediately reported to relevant authorities.

🚫 Hate Speech

No content that attacks, demeans, or incites violence against individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or nationality.

🚫 Cyberbullying & Harassment

No targeted harassment, threats, doxxing, or repeated unwanted contact. This includes coordinated campaigns to demean or intimidate other users.

🚫 Violence & Self-Harm

No content that promotes, glorifies, or provides instructions for self-harm, suicide, eating disorders, or acts of violence. We provide resources and support for users at risk.

🚫 Copyright Infringement

No unauthorized use of copyrighted music, video, or images. Repeated infringement will result in account termination under our repeat-infringer policy.

🚫 Spam & Scams

No fake engagement (bots, bought followers/likes), phishing links, fraudulent giveaways, or deceptive commercial content.

🚫 Impersonation

No creating fake profiles that impersonate other individuals, public figures, or brands. Accounts that mislead others about their true identity or affiliation will be removed.

🚫 Dangerous Misinformation

No spreading misleading false information or harmful rumors that could deceive the public or cause harm to society.

🎙️ These rules apply fully to Voice & Audio Clips

Voice posts, voice replies, and audio backgrounds are held to the exact same standard as videos and photos. Using a voice clip to hurl abusive language, make threats, spread hate speech, or harass another user is strictly prohibited — audio-only content is not a loophole. Reports on voice posts are reviewed by listening to the actual clip, and violations here carry the same strike consequences as any other content type.

2Location & Nearby Feature Safety

VibeFlick's Nearby feature shows you thoughts and posts from people physically close to you, based on your location. Because this feature involves real-world proximity, we hold it to an extra-strict safety standard.

🚷 Stalking & Harassment

Using the Nearby feature to track, follow, monitor, or repeatedly show up around another user in real life is strictly prohibited. Using proximity information to intimidate, harass, or pressure someone is a severe violation and may result in an immediate permanent ban.

🔍 Doxxing & Privacy

Publicly sharing another person's home address, workplace, daily routine, exact location, or other personal identifying information without their explicit consent is strictly prohibited — whether in a Nearby post, comment, voice clip, or direct message.

🕵️ Anonymous Misuse

Anonymous Vibe posts in Nearby are meant for honest, low-pressure sharing — not as cover for harassment, threats, or revealing someone else's identity or location without consent. Anonymity does not exempt anyone from these guidelines.

📍 Location Accuracy

Don't manipulate or spoof your location to gain unfair reach, impersonate presence in a place you're not in, or evade a location-based restriction or ban.

⚠️

Safety Disclaimer: VibeFlick's Nearby feature is designed to help you discover content from people around you, not to arrange in-person meetings. If you choose to meet someone you connected with through VibeFlick, you do so entirely at your own risk. Always meet in public places, tell a friend or family member where you're going, and trust your instincts. VibeFlick is not responsible for interactions that happen outside the app, and users are solely responsible for their own personal safety when meeting others in real life.

3User Safety Features

VibeFlick provides built-in tools so you can control your experience and help keep the community safe.

🚩 In-App Reporting

Report any post, comment, voice clip, message, Nearby thought, or profile directly from within the app. Our Trust & Safety team reviews all reports and typically responds within 24 hours.

🔒 Blocking

Block any user to instantly stop them from viewing your profile, seeing your Nearby posts, contacting you, or interacting with your content. Blocked users are not notified.

🔕 Mute & Restrict

Mute accounts to hide their content without unfollowing, or restrict comments from specific users without them knowing.

🔐 Privacy Controls

Set your account to private, control who can message you, choose who sees your Nearby posts, and manage who can tag or mention you.

4For You Feed & Recommendations

The For You feed uses an automated recommendation system to surface videos, photos, and voice posts we think you'll enjoy, based on signals like watch time, likes, and shares.

🛡️ Borderline & violating content is filtered out of recommendations

Content that violates these Community Guidelines is removed from the platform. Content that sits in a borderline or sensitive category — without clearly breaking a rule — is not amplified through the For You recommendation system, even if it remains visible on the creator's own profile. Our filtering happens automatically before a post is eligible for recommendation, and is continuously reviewed and improved.

This means reach on VibeFlick is earned by creating positive, rule-following content — not by pushing the boundaries of what's allowed. If you believe your content was filtered from recommendations in error, you can reach out using the Appeal Process below.

5Account Enforcement Policy

VibeFlick uses a progressive strike system for most violations — across videos, photos, voice clips, Nearby posts, and comments alike. Severe violations (such as content involving child exploitation, credible threats of violence, stalking, or terrorism) may result in an immediate permanent ban without prior warning.

1

Strike 1 — Account Warning

A first-time or minor violation results in content removal and a formal warning notice on your account. No posting restrictions apply, but repeated violations will escalate to Strike 2.

2

Strike 2 — Temporary Posting Restriction

A second violation results in a temporary restriction on posting, commenting, and messaging, typically lasting 3–14 days depending on severity. Your existing content remains visible unless it violates guidelines.

3

Strike 3 — Permanent Account Ban

A third violation, or a single severe violation, results in permanent suspension of your VibeFlick account. Banned users may not create new accounts to circumvent this action.

Note: Strikes may expire after 90 days of good standing for minor (Strike 1) violations, at VibeFlick's discretion. Strike expiration does not apply to severe or repeated violations, including stalking, doxxing, or harassment through the Nearby feature.

6Appeal Process

If you believe an enforcement action was taken in error, you have the right to appeal.

📱 Appeal via the App

Go to Inbox → Account Notices, select the notice in question, and tap "Appeal this decision." Provide any context that supports your appeal. Our team reviews appeals within 24–72 hours.

✉️ Appeal via Email

Email support@vibeflick.com with your username, the notice/strike details, and an explanation. Include screenshots if available to help us investigate faster.

Decisions on permanent bans arising from severe violations (e.g., child safety, terrorism, credible threats, stalking) are final and not eligible for appeal.
