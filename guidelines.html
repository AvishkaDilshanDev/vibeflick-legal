VibeFlick Community Guidelines

Building a safe, respectful, and creative community

You must be at least 13 years old (or 18, where required by local law) to use VibeFlick.

Welcome to VibeFlick! These Community Guidelines exist to keep our platform safe, respectful, and enjoyable for everyone who shares and discovers videos, photos, voice clips, thoughts, and rewards here. By using VibeFlick, you agree to follow these guidelines in addition to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

We want VibeFlick to be a place where creativity thrives without fear of harassment, hate, or harm. Every rule below exists to protect our community — please take a moment to read through them.

1Prohibited Content

The following types of content are strictly prohibited on VibeFlick — whether posted as a video, photo, voice clip, thought, or comment. Violations may result in content removal, account restrictions, or permanent bans, depending on severity.

🚫 Nudity & Explicit Content

No sexually explicit content, pornography, or non-consensual intimate imagery. This includes nudity involving minors in any form, which will be immediately reported to relevant authorities.

🚫 Hate Speech

No content that attacks, demeans, or incites violence against individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or nationality.

🚫 Cyberbullying & Harassment

No targeted harassment, threats, doxxing, or repeated unwanted contact. This includes coordinated campaigns to demean or intimidate other users.

🚫 Violence & Self-Harm

No content that promotes, glorifies, or provides instructions for self-harm, suicide, eating disorders, or acts of violence. We provide resources and support for users at risk.

🚫 Copyright Infringement

No unauthorized use of copyrighted music, video, or images. Repeated infringement will result in account termination under our repeat-infringer policy.

🚫 Spam & Scams

No fake engagement (bots, bought followers/likes), phishing links, fraudulent giveaways, or deceptive commercial content.

🚫 Impersonation

No creating fake profiles that impersonate other individuals, public figures, or brands. Accounts that mislead others about their true identity or affiliation will be removed.

🚫 Dangerous Misinformation

No spreading misleading false information or harmful rumors that could deceive the public or cause harm to society.

🎙️ These rules apply fully to Voice & Audio Clips

Voice posts, voice replies, and audio backgrounds are held to the exact same standard as videos and photos. Using a voice clip to hurl abusive language, make threats, spread hate speech, or harass another user is strictly prohibited — audio-only content is not a loophole. Reports on voice posts are reviewed by listening to the actual clip, and violations here carry the same strike consequences as any other content type.

3For You Feed & Recommendations

The For You feed uses an automated recommendation system to surface videos, photos, and voice posts we think you'll enjoy, based on signals like watch time, likes, and shares.

🛡️ Borderline & violating content is filtered out of recommendations

Content that violates these Community Guidelines is removed from the platform. Content that sits in a borderline or sensitive category — without clearly breaking a rule — is not amplified through the For You recommendation system, even if it remains visible on the creator's own profile. Our filtering happens automatically before a post is eligible for recommendation, and is continuously reviewed and improved.

This means reach on VibeFlick is earned by creating positive, rule-following content — not by pushing the boundaries of what's allowed. If you believe your content was filtered from recommendations in error, you can reach out using the Appeal Process below.

4Account Enforcement Policy

VibeFlick uses a progressive strike system for most violations — across videos, photos, voice clips, Nearby posts, comments, and rewards or gifting activity alike. Severe violations (such as content involving child exploitation, credible threats of violence, stalking, or terrorism) may result in an immediate permanent ban without prior warning.

1

Strike 1 — Account Warning

A first-time or minor violation results in content removal and a formal warning notice on your account. No posting restrictions apply, but repeated violations will escalate to Strike 2.

2

Strike 2 — Temporary Posting Restriction

A second violation results in a temporary restriction on posting, commenting, and messaging, typically lasting 3–14 days depending on severity. Your existing content remains visible unless it violates guidelines.

3

Strike 3 — Permanent Account Ban

A third violation, or a single severe violation, results in permanent suspension of your VibeFlick account. Banned users may not create new accounts to circumvent this action, and the device used may also be blocked from creating new accounts.

Note: Strikes may expire after 90 days of good standing for minor (Strike 1) violations, at VibeFlick's discretion. Strike expiration does not apply to severe or repeated violations, including stalking, doxxing, or harassment through the Nearby feature.

5Appeal Process

If you believe an enforcement action was taken in error, you have the right to appeal.

📱 Appeal via the App

Go to Inbox → Account Notices, select the notice in question, and tap "Appeal this decision." Provide any context that supports your appeal. Our team reviews appeals within 24–72 hours.

✉️ Appeal via Email

Email support@vibeflick.com with your username, the notice/strike details, and an explanation. Include screenshots if available to help us investigate faster.

Decisions on permanent bans arising from severe violations (e.g., child safety, terrorism, credible threats, stalking) are final and not eligible for appeal.

6Rewards, Leaderboard & Gifting

VibeFlick lets you earn VCoins through tasks and daily check-ins, compete on the Leaderboard, and send Virtual Gifts to creators. Rewards are meant for people who genuinely enjoy the community. All rules in Sections 1 to 5 also apply to everything you do while earning, competing, or gifting. Taking part in the rewards features is optional, and no purchase is needed to earn VCoins or appear on the Leaderboard. See our Terms and Conditions (Leaderboard and Rewards Terms) for full details.

🚫 Spam & Fake Activity

Do not post repeated letters, words, or meaningless comments (for example "aaaaa", "hihihi", or "....."), or copy-paste the same comment on many posts, just to earn VCoins. Rewards are given only for genuine activity.

🤖 Bots, Auto-Clickers & Emulators

Using bots, scripts, auto-clickers, macros, emulators, or any automation to watch, like, comment, share, check in, or invite is prohibited. Rewards must come from a real person using the real app on a real device.

👥 Fake Accounts & Referral Abuse

Do not create fake, duplicate, or bot accounts, or invite your own other accounts, to collect invitation rewards. Referrals made with the same device, or to yourself, are not valid. The number of accounts that can be created from one device is limited.

🐞 Exploits & Trading

Do not abuse bugs or errors to gain VCoins, and do not sell, buy, or trade accounts, VCoins, ranks, or prizes. If you find a bug, please report it to us instead of using it.

Fair Leaderboard Competition

The Leaderboard must be a fair competition for everyone. Anyone who uses cheating methods to climb the Leaderboard, including bots, automation, fake or duplicate accounts, exploiting bugs, using stolen or unauthorized payment methods, or any other unfair method, may face the following actions, depending on severity:

  • Removal of the VCoins gained unfairly, up to a full reset of your VCoin balance and rank, and removal from the Leaderboard.
  • Loss of any prize or payout connected to the cheating, and disqualification of the prize in favor of the next eligible user.
  • A strike on your account under Section 4, or a permanent ban of the account.
  • For serious or repeated cheating, a ban on the device used, so that new accounts cannot be created from that device to continue cheating.

You never need to buy anything to climb the Leaderboard or to be eligible for a prize. VCoins earned for free count the same as purchased VCoins. Rewards are based on activity and ranking only, with no element of chance, and VCoins have no cash value. If you are under 18 (or the age of majority where you live), purchases and subscriptions are allowed only with a parent's or guardian's permission.

Buying VCoins or a Rank Boost is optional and does not guarantee a rank or a prize. Purchases made with stolen or unauthorized payment methods will be reversed, and the account may be banned.

💬 Respectful Gifting & Interaction

Virtual Gifts and comments must be sent in a respectful way. Do not send gifts together with, or in return for, abusive, obscene, sexual, or threatening messages or comments. Do not use gifts to pressure, harass, bribe, or manipulate a creator or another user, or to demand personal information, private photos, or meetings. Gifts must not be used for scams, fraud, money laundering, or to move value to another account. Obscene or threatening comments and gift messages are treated like any other violation of Section 1 and may lead to removal, a strike, or a ban.

Gifts, once sent, cannot be undone, and may be removed or reversed if they were sent as part of a violation. Creator payouts (Cash-out) are available only to eligible VibeFlick Plus members with an account in good standing, and may be withheld or rejected if we find fraud, fake gifting, or other rule violations.

📣 Reporting Cheating

If you see someone cheating on the Leaderboard or misusing gifts, use the in-app Report option or contact us at support@vibeflick.com. Submitting false reports is also a violation of these guidelines. If you think a reward action was taken against you by mistake, you can appeal as described in Section 5.

7GIFTS, COINS & CREATOR EARNINGS

VibeFlick lets you support creators by sending virtual gifts on posts. This section explains how gifts work and what is not allowed.

How gifts work

  • Gifts are virtual items you send using Coins. Coins are bought through Google Play.
  • Prices are shown in the app before you send. A daily spending limit applies.
  • A gift cannot be taken back once it is sent. Gifts have no cash value to the sender and are not donations, investments or payment for goods or services.
  • Creators receive "Diamonds" for the gifts they get. Diamonds can be cashed out only under our Terms and Conditions (VibeFlick Plus members, minimum amount, manual review).
  • Sending or receiving gifts does not exempt anyone from these Community Guidelines and does not change how content is reviewed.

What is not allowed

1. Gift farming

Sending gifts between your own, linked or friends' accounts (or using multiple accounts or devices) to create Diamonds, earnings or leaderboard activity.

2. Pressure and manipulation

Demanding or begging for gifts, threatening or guilt-tripping people, or promising followers, prizes, rewards, favours or personal contact in return for gifts.

3. Gifts for prohibited content

Offering, requesting or "unlocking" sexual, violent, dangerous, humiliating or otherwise prohibited content or acts in exchange for gifts. Any such content involving minors is reported to the authorities.

4. Scams

Fake giveaways, fake emergencies or charities, impersonation, phishing, or asking people to send money or gifts to "win", "unlock" or "get coins back".

5. Selling or trading

Selling, buying, trading or transferring Coins, Diamonds, gifts or accounts, or arranging payments outside the app.

6. Payment abuse

Using stolen or unauthorized payment methods, abusing refunds or chargebacks, or exploiting bugs.

7. Gambling-style activity

Raffles, lotteries or games of chance that use gifts or Coins to enter or win.

8. Harassment

Using gifts to target, harass or embarrass someone.

9. Misleading claims

Promising guaranteed income, claiming payouts are official VibeFlick salaries, or misrepresenting how gifts or Diamonds work.

Minors

  • If you are under 18 (or the age of majority where you live), you may buy Coins only with a parent or guardian's permission.
  • Cash-out for users under 18 requires a parent or guardian's consent.

Enforcement

If we find a violation, we may remove the content and the related gifts or Diamonds, withhold or reject payouts, restrict gifting or cash-out, issue a strike (Strike 1, 2 or 3), or permanently ban the account and block the device. Cash-out requests are reviewed manually and may be rejected, for example for fraud, policy breaches or incorrect details.

📣 Report a problem

Use the Report option in the app, or email officialvibeflickgx@gmail.com.

8VibeFlick LIVE

VibeFlick LIVE lets eligible creators broadcast real-time video and audio, and lets viewers watch, comment, like, and send Virtual Gifts. Everything shown or said in a live is visible to anyone who joins. All rules in Sections 1 to 5 apply fully to LIVE, including what appears on camera, what is heard through the microphone, comments, gifts, and guests, in any language. Because live content happens in real time, we act quickly when something is reported.

Going live

  • Going live requires camera and microphone permission and a minimum number of followers. If you do not meet the requirement, the app tells you how many more followers you need.
  • Accounts that are banned, scheduled for deletion, or under a posting restriction cannot go live.
  • You can choose whether to notify your followers when you start. Follower notifications may be limited to prevent spam.
  • Only one live per account can run at a time, and a live can last up to 3 hours.
  • A live ends automatically if nobody joins for about 12 minutes (a warning appears at 10 minutes), or if the host leaves the app in the background for more than 5 minutes.
  • Repeatedly starting and ending very short lives within an hour may temporarily pause the Go Live feature for that account.

What is not allowed in a LIVE

🚫 Nudity & Sexual Content

No nudity, sexual acts, or sexually suggestive behavior on camera, in audio, or on screen. Content involving minors is reported to the relevant authorities.

🚫 Violence & Dangerous Acts

No violence, threats, weapons used to intimidate, or dangerous stunts and challenges. Do not show, encourage, or glorify self-harm or suicide. If you or someone near you is in danger, contact your local emergency services first.

🚫 Hate & Harassment

No hate speech, bullying, threats, or humiliating another person on air. Abusive or obscene comments are also prohibited.

🧒 Child Safety

No content that sexualizes, exploits, or endangers minors. Do not ask minors for personal details, private contact, photos, or meetings.

🔐 Privacy

Do not show or share another person's personal information, and do not film people in private settings without their consent.

🚫 Scams, Spam & Illegal Activity

No fake giveaways, phishing, gambling, selling illegal goods, misleading claims, or fake engagement (bots, repeated copy-paste comments).

©️ Copyright

Do not broadcast movies, TV, matches, music, or other content you do not have the right to share.

🎁 Pressure for Gifts

Do not beg for gifts, pressure viewers, or promise favors, personal contact, or prohibited acts in return for gifts. All rules in Section 7 apply to gifts sent in a live.

🚷 Evasion

Do not use another account to rejoin a live after being removed, or use a misleading live title to attract viewers.

Guests

A host may invite up to 3 viewers to join the live as guests. A viewer is only added after accepting the invitation, and is told that their camera and microphone will be visible to everyone in the live. A guest can leave at any time and the host can remove a guest at any time. Hosts are responsible for the content of their live, including the guests they invite, and guests must follow all of these guidelines.

Safety tools

🚩 Report a Live

Tap ⋮ More → Report in any live, choose a reason, and add optional details. You can also block the creator while reporting. Each user can report a given live once.

🔒 Block & Mute

Block a creator to stop seeing their lives and prevent them from interacting with you. You can also mute a host's audio on your own device.

🛡️ Host Controls

Hosts can turn off a viewer's comments, remove a viewer (who cannot rejoin that live), hide a comment from their own view, and remove a guest.

⏱️ Comment Limits

To reduce spam, comments are rate-limited, and sending the same comment repeatedly triggers a short waiting period.

Gifts in LIVE

  • Virtual Gifts are sent using Coins. Prices are shown in the app before you send, and a daily spending limit applies.
  • A gift cannot be taken back once it is sent, has no cash value to the sender, and is not a donation, investment, or payment for goods or services.
  • Creators receive Diamonds for gifts, which can be cashed out only under our Terms and Conditions (see Section 7).
  • Sending or receiving gifts does not exempt anyone from these guidelines.

⚖️ How we enforce these rules in LIVE

Our team reviews reports about lives. Depending on severity, we may end a live immediately, remove the host from the room, remove related gifts or payouts, issue a Strike 1, 2, or 3 under Section 4, restrict Go Live, or permanently ban the account and device. Severe violations (such as child exploitation, sexual content, credible threats of violence, or terrorism) may result in an immediate permanent ban, which is not eligible for appeal (see Section 5). Ending a live yourself does not remove a report or a strike that has already been issued. Submitting false reports is also a violation.

⚠️

Safety Disclaimer: Live content is broadcast in real time and cannot be reviewed before it airs, so please use the Report option if you see something that breaks these guidelines. In-app reporting is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services. Questions can be sent to support@vibeflick.com.
