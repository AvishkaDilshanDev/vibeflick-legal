Quick safety checklist

  • Never share your password, 2-step PIN or any verification code with anyone, including people who say they work for VibeFlick.
  • Turn on 2-Step PIN Verification and check Logged In Devices now and then.
  • Buy VCoins and subscriptions only inside the app, through Google Play.
  • Do not post phone numbers, addresses or ID documents, yours or anyone else’s.
  • Report anything that looks like a scam, harassment or a fake account.
