What is VibeFlick?

VibeFlick is a social app for short-form creativity. Record a video, write a poem, share a thought, post a photo, or drop a voice clip. Follow the creators you like, react, comment, and build a feed that feels like you.

It is built to be simple, friendly and fair: no pay-to-win, clear rules, and tools that keep you in control of your own experience.

Our story
VibeFlick was built by one person, from scratch, with a simple idea: a social app where everyday people can share what they love, get noticed, and have a good time doing it.

We care about real creativity and real people. That is why the app has clear community rules, in-app reporting and blocking, and a rewards system that does not require spending money. We do not allow scams, fake engagement or harassment, and we work to keep the community safe and welcoming.

What you can do

Short videos

Create, upload and share short videos, and discover new creators in your For You feed.

Posts and voice clips

Share text posts, colored text cards, image posts with hashtags and categories like Poetry, Story and Motivation, plus voice clips.

Nearby

See thoughts from people close to you, with strict rules against stalking, doxxing and harassment.

Interactive Leaderboard

Earn VCoins by checking in daily and joining in. Climb the Today and All-time rankings and compete for the daily prizes shown in the app. No purchase is needed to take part.

VCoins and gifts

Use VCoins to send virtual gifts to creators you enjoy. Optional VCoin packs are bought through Google Play. VCoins have no cash value.

VibeFlick Plus

An optional subscription with bigger reward amounts, a higher daily cap, a premium badge, profile styles and cash-out access for eligible members.

Safety tools

Report, block, mute and privacy controls are built in, and every report is reviewed.

Fair by design

Rewards are based only on activity and ranking, with no element of chance. Cheating, bots and fake accounts are not allowed.

Delete your account and data

You can delete your VibeFlick account and the data linked to it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open VibeFlick and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Without the app

Email officialvibeflickgx@gmail.com from the address on your account, with the subject “Account deletion request” and your username. We will verify and process your request.

What happens: your account and personal information are removed from our active databases. We may keep limited records where needed to prevent fraud and ban evasion, handle disputes, and meet legal duties, as explained in our Privacy Policy. Content you posted is removed when you delete your account.
