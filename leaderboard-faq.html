Leaderboard & Rewards FAQ

Everything about earning VCoins, climbing the ranks and winning daily prizes on VibeFlick.

Leaderboard

What is the Leaderboard?

The Leaderboard ranks users by how many VCoins they earn. It has two views: Today and All-time. The top 50 users are listed, and your own rank is always shown to you.

What is the difference between Today and All-time?
  • Today counts only VCoins added on the current day. It resets at midnight, Sri Lanka time (Asia/Colombo).
  • All-time counts every VCoin added to your account since you joined.
What counts towards my rank?

VCoins credited to you: daily check-ins, tasks, friend invitations and VCoins you buy. Gifts you send do not add to your rank.

What happens if two users have the same VCoins?

The tie is broken automatically by our system, so every user always has a single, unique rank.

Why did my rank not change right away?

The Leaderboard refreshes periodically, so it can lag by a short time. Ranks can change at any moment as other users earn VCoins.

What can other users see about me?

If you earn VCoins, your username, profile picture, verified badge (if any), VCoin total, rank and points can appear on the Leaderboard. Others can also open a limited Progress view with your daily VCoin totals and points.

They cannot see which posts you viewed or the specific activities you performed.

What is the Progress view?

It shows a user's recent days: VCoins earned and points for each day. Your own Progress panel shows your last 7 days and your total VCoins. Tap a user on the Leaderboard to view theirs, three days at a time.

How are winners chosen?

How will I know if I won?

Winners get an in-app notification and an email to their account address. Keep your email accurate and check your notifications.

Winners under 18 need a parent or guardian's consent. Prizes are non-transferable, have no cash alternative, and winners are responsible for any taxes or fees.

Can a prize be taken away?

Yes. If we reasonably believe the rules were broken, we can disqualify the user and award the prize to the next eligible user.

VCoins & Wallet

What are VCoins (PCoins)?

Virtual points that exist only inside VibeFlick. They are not money, have no cash value and cannot be sold or traded outside the app. VCoins and PCoins are the same thing.

What does “pending” mean?

Coins from tasks first appear as pending. Swipe the “To Wallet” slider to move them into your Wallet. Check-in rewards, the profile and first-upload bonuses, invitations and purchases go straight to your Wallet.

Can my pending coins expire?

If you do not open VibeFlick for 20 days, your pending VCoins may reset. VCoins already in your Wallet are safe.

My balance looks different from what I expected.

The balance is based on our server records. If there is a difference, our records prevail. Pull down to refresh, or contact us if it still looks wrong.

Amounts shown in the app at the time you act are the ones that apply.

Is there a daily cap?

Yes. Task earnings are capped at 500 VCoins per day for free accounts and 5,000 for VibeFlick Plus members. Once you reach it, more task activity that day earns nothing extra. The one-time profile bonus is not part of the cap. Check-ins and invitations are separate.

Why didn't I get coins for a task?

Rewards are verified by our servers. Common reasons:

  • You already earned from that post (each post rewards once).
  • It was your own post (liking or commenting on your own posts earns nothing).
  • The comment was too short, repeated characters or posted too long ago. Comments must be genuine and recent.
  • You reached the daily limit for that task, or the overall daily cap.
What do I need for the profile bonus?

Every profile field must be filled in: name, username, email, bio, birthday, region, profile photo and cover photo. The app tells you which one is missing.

Inviting friends

How does inviting work?

Share your invite link from Invite Friends. When a new user installs through it and signs up, they are recorded as a pending referral. You both receive 200 VCoins (2,000 for you if you are a Plus member) after the new user claims their first daily check-in.

Why was my invitation not rewarded?

A referral is not valid if the account is not genuinely new, if both accounts use the same device, or if you invite yourself. A limit on paid invitations per 24 hours also applies, and any above it may not be rewarded.

Buying VCoins & Rank Boost

Do I need to buy anything to rank?

No. Everything is optional, and you can earn VCoins and appear on the Leaderboard for free.

What is Rank Boost?

An optional purchase that adds the VCoins needed to reach the target rank shown at the time you buy (with a minimum amount). It does not guarantee a rank or a prize, because other users keep earning and ranks can change afterwards. It only appears when you are below the target.

I was charged but got no VCoins.

Purchases are verified with Google Play before coins are added. If your connection dropped, the app retries automatically the next time you open Buy PCoins. If nothing arrives, email us your Google Play order details.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are final except where required by law or Google Play policy. If Google Play refunds or reverses a purchase, the related VCoins may be removed from your balance (never below zero).

  • Higher task rewards (5×) and check-in rewards (5×)

Gifts & Cash-out

How do gifts work?

You can spend VCoins on virtual gifts for creators. Prices are shown in the app, a daily spending limit applies, and a sent gift cannot be undone. Creators receive diamonds, and they can see who sent the gift.

How do I cash out?
  • Cash-out is for Plus members with an account in good standing.
  • You need at least US$10.00 worth of diamonds.
  • Pay-out options: bank, PayPal, Payoneer or wallet.
  • Requests are reviewed manually. If rejected, your diamonds are returned.
  • Only one request can be pending at a time.

The amount paid is the creator's share shown in the app. The rest covers platform and store fees. You are responsible for any taxes.

Fair play

What is not allowed?
  • Bots, scripts, emulators or any automation to earn VCoins
  • Multiple or fake accounts (the number of accounts per device is limited)
  • Fake or repetitive likes, comments or views
  • Abusing invitations or exploiting bugs
  • Trading or selling accounts, VCoins or ranks
  • Using a stolen or unauthorised payment method
What happens if I break the rules?

We may remove VCoins and rewards, reset your streak, remove you from the Leaderboard, withhold prizes or payouts, restrict or suspend the account and block the device. Serious or repeated violations of the Community Guidelines can lead to strikes and a permanent ban.

Can the Rewards Program change?

Yes. Reward amounts, limits, prizes and terms can change, and updates are posted in our Terms. We will not unfairly remove coins you already received legitimately.
