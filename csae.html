Our Zero-Tolerance Commitment

VibeFlick maintains an absolute zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form. We are committed to protecting every child from harm on our platform and cooperating fully with law enforcement and regulatory authorities worldwide.

Our platform is intended for users aged 13 and older (or 18 where required by local law). Content involving the sexualisation, abuse, or exploitation of minors will be removed immediately and, where required, reported to the relevant national authorities and the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

Absolute policy: Any account found to upload, distribute, or solicit CSAE material will be permanently banned and reported to law enforcement — no exceptions, no appeals.
2

Prohibited Content & Behaviour

The following are strictly prohibited on VibeFlick. Violations result in immediate permanent account termination and referral to authorities.

🚫 Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)

Any image, video, audio, or text that sexually depicts, exploits, or abuses a minor — including AI-generated or cartoon content.

🚫 Grooming Behaviour

Attempts to build inappropriate relationships with minors, including via direct messages, comments, or the Nearby feature.

🚫 Solicitation of Minors

Requesting sexual content, personal information, or offline meetings from anyone who is or appears to be a minor.

🚫 Predatory Conduct

Using any VibeFlick feature — including Nearby, voice clips, or private messages — to contact, target, or exploit minors.

🚫 Sexualised Minor Content

Content that sexualises minors in any way, even without explicit imagery — including suggestive captions, descriptions, or role-play.

🚫 Facilitating CSAE

Sharing links, codes, contacts, or any information that could be used to access, distribute, or facilitate child exploitation material.

3

Detection & Enforcement

VibeFlick uses a combination of automated technology and human review to detect and remove CSAE content.

🔍

Automated Hash Matching

We use PhotoDNA and industry-standard hash-matching technology to detect known CSAM before it is visible to any user.

👥

Human Trust & Safety Review

Reported content is reviewed by trained Trust & Safety team members who assess context and severity within 24 hours.

🚨

Immediate Removal & Account Action

Violating content is removed immediately. Accounts are permanently banned. Evidence is preserved for law enforcement.

📋

Authority Reporting

All confirmed CSAM is reported to NCMEC's CyberTipline and relevant national authorities as required by law, including in Sri Lanka, the US, and the EU.

4

How to Report

If you see content or behaviour on VibeFlick that may involve child exploitation or abuse, please report it immediately using one of the methods below.

📱 Report In-App

Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) on any post, comment, profile, or message → select Report → choose Child Safety. Reports are reviewed by our team within 24 hours.

✉️ Report by Email

Send a detailed report — including the username, content link, and description — to officialvibeflickgx@gmail.com with the subject line "Child Safety Report." We treat all such reports as urgent.

🔒 Your Report Is Confidential

Reports are kept confidential. The reported user is never informed of who reported them. You will receive a confirmation response within 24–72 hours.

5

Reporting to Authorities

You can also report child exploitation directly to the relevant national or international authority. VibeFlick complies fully with all authority investigations and legal requests.

🌐 NCMEC CyberTipline (Global)

  • Website: cybertipline.org
  • Accepts reports of online child sexual exploitation from anywhere in the world.

🇱🇰 Sri Lanka — National Child Protection Authority

  • Hotline: 1929
  • Website: ncpa.gov.lk
  • For reports within Sri Lanka, you may also contact the Sri Lanka Police Child Protection Unit.

🇪🇺 EU / International

  • Report to your country's national law enforcement or the Internet Watch Foundation (IWF) at iwf.org.uk.
VibeFlick cooperates fully with law enforcement and will provide all requested data in connection with child safety investigations, as required by applicable law.
